Everything your kennel needs, in one place.

Pedigrees, health tests, litters, puppy buyers and finances for dog breeders. It runs on your own Windows computer, so your records stay yours.

Install Kennel Studio

Windows 10 or 11 · 7-day free trial · updates itself

What's inside

Dogs and pedigrees

A record for every dog with photos, registration details and stud book numbers, and printable pedigrees.

Breeding and COI

Check the coefficient of inbreeding before a mating, compare possible matches and keep a stud and mating log.

Health tests and certificates

Hips, eyes, DNA results and more, with reminders and printable certificates.

Litters and puppies

From planned mating to the day each puppy goes home, with weight tracking and worming schedules.

Whelping log

Temperature tracking, birth times, seasons and progesterone tests in one timeline.

Socialisation and puppy packs

Checklists for every puppy and a pack of advice and records for new owners.

Buyers and appointments

A waiting list, viewings and collections, plus the dogs each client already has.

Contracts and finance

Contract templates, deposits and balances, and a simple ledger with a plan for each litter.

More than one kennel

Switch between kennel profiles without mixing up the records.

Install in a minute

  1. Click Install Kennel Studio above and open the file when it downloads.
  2. If Windows shows a blue "protected your PC" screen, choose More info, then Run anyway.
  3. Start Kennel Studio from the Start menu. It checks for updates each time it opens.
  4. Sign in with Google or Microsoft from the Licence and account screen when you are ready to buy a licence.
